Common questions

How much does an EV charger installation cost?

Basic EV charger installs start at $600, and more complex ones are around $1,200. The final price depends on how far the charger is from your panel, how much capacity the panel has, and whether the wiring runs through finished walls or underground. We check all of that at the site assessment and give you a fixed, written quote before any work starts.

Do you install EV chargers in Winder, GA?

Yes. Tails Electric installs home and commercial EV chargers in Winder and the surrounding Barrow County area.

How long does an EV charger installation take?

Most home installs take 1 to 4 hours depending on complexity. Long wiring runs, panel work, or finished walls can add time.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes. We handle the permit and schedule the inspection so you don't have to chase either one.

Do I need a panel upgrade for a Level 2 charger?

Not always. We check your panel's capacity before we quote. If it can't carry a new 240V circuit, we quote the upgrade up front.
