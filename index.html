09 — FAQ

Common questions

Didn't see your question? Ask us directly when you request a quote.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry insurance and all electrical work is overseen by a licensed electrical contractor.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, every job starts with a free, no-obligation quote and a written estimate before we touch a wire.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Braselton and cover roughly a 20-mile radius — see the full list of towns and map above. Not sure if you're in range? Send us your address when you request a quote and we'll confirm.

How fast can you get to a job?

Our average response time is under 24 hours, and we prioritize anything that sounds like a safety risk.

Do you pull permits?

Yes. We handle the permit and schedule the inspection so you don't have to chase either one.

How much does an EV charger installation cost?

Basic EV charger installs start at $600, and more complex ones are around $1,200. The final price depends on how far the charger is from your panel, how much capacity the panel has, and whether the wiring runs through finished walls or underground. We check all of that at the site assessment and give you a fixed, written quote before any work starts.

Do I need a panel upgrade for a Level 2 charger?

Not always. We check your panel's capacity before we quote. If it can't carry a new 240V circuit, we quote the upgrade up front, and most home installs take 1 to 4 hours depending on complexity.

Which EV chargers do you install?

We install Level 2 chargers for every major EV, including Tesla, Ford, Rivian, GM, and Hyundai/Kia, with either NACS or J1772 connectors.

Are there rebates for EV charger installation?

Georgia Power customers may qualify for a rebate on an eligible Level 2 charger. Check georgiapower.com/evrebate for current terms. If your power comes from an EMC or a city utility, ask them about their programs, since each one sets its own.
